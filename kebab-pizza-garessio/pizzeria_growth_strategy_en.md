# Growth and Negotiation Strategy: Horus Pizza & Kebab
*A Complete Guide for Vasile: Technical Roadmap, Marketing, and Pricing Models*

This document summarizes all the ideas, features, and business strategies discussed for the **Horus Pizza & Kebab** project (covering both Garessio and Ceva locations). You can use this as a reference guide during tonight's meeting and for subsequent negotiations.

---

## 1. Current Prototype Status (Garessio)
We have built a premium, fully mobile-responsive web prototype available in two layouts:
* **Standard Layout (`index.html`)**: Clean street-food design on a dark background, with high-quality images shown only for key flagship items.
* **Transparent Parallax Layout (`index_bg.html`)**: A stunning visual effect with a fixed wood-fired oven background image (`background-attachment: fixed`), overlaid with transparent, frosted-glass menu cards (glassmorphism) that float during scroll.

### Key Refinements Already Implemented:
* **Menu Organization Matching the Flyer**: The menu categorizes pizzas exactly like the physical flyer. The *Pizze Bianche* section starts precisely with *Biancaneve* and continues downward, while all tomato-sauce pizzas above it are placed under *Classiche*.
* **Authentic Food Graphics**:
  * *Pizza Diavola*: Wood-fired rustic crust, simple melted mozzarella, tomato sauce, and flat, circular slices of spicy Italian salami (no green herbs/basil, with the red chili pepper fully visible next to the board).
  * *Pizza Capricciosa*: Cotto ham, mushrooms, black olives, and strips of red and yellow bell peppers on a wood-fired crust (matching the real pizza photo from their box).
  * *Panino Kebab*: Set on a wooden cutting board from a three-quarter profile angle to showcase the filling, depth, and volume (no hand).
  * *Piatto Kebab* and *Menu Hamburger*: Integrated realistic, high-impact food photography.
* **Discrete Spicy Badges**: Replaced bright red blocks with an elegant, semi-transparent dark-grey rounded pill (`🌶️`) featuring a thin border.
* **Smart Search Engine**: The search bar now matches inside badge texts (e.g., searching for "picante", "iute", "spicy", or "🌶️" displays all spicy items; searching for "consigliata" displays Pizza Kebab and Piatto Kebab).

## 2. Phase 1: Free Trial Period (Month 1)
To gain their complete trust (zero-risk strategy for the business owner), you offer the first month free under the following conditions:

### Directly Attributed Lead Tracking (WhatsApp Attribution Prefix)
Every order sent through the website automatically generates a WhatsApp message starting with:
`"Ciao! Vorrei ordinare tramite il vostro sito web..."`
This ensures the owners see directly on their phones that every single web order was generated by your website.

### Value-Added Services Included in the Trial:
* **Google Maps Claiming & Optimization**: You handle claiming and configuring the Google Business Profiles for both locations (Garessio and Ceva).
* **"Website" Link on Google Maps**: You link the new website directly to their Google Maps buttons (the main source of local customer traffic in Italy).
* **Menu QR Code**: A high-resolution QR code (`assets/qr_menu.png`) for their windows/tables, opening the menu instantly on clients' phones.

## 3. Phase 2: Premium Expansion Package (The Upsells)
After the one-month trial, once you have proven that the website brings in real orders and value, you propose the following premium upgrades:

### A. The Franchise Portal (Garessio + Ceva)
* A single domain name (e.g., `horuspizzerie.it`) with a landing page where customers choose their location: **Garessio** or **Ceva**.
* The Ceva menu will be customized to include the **MAXI Pizza** columns (which are not available in Garessio), along with the respective phone numbers and WhatsApp links for Ceva.

### B. Interactive Shopping Cart (E-Commerce Flow)
* Customers can add multiple items to a digital cart (e.g., 2 Kebabs, 1 Fanta, 1 Tiramisu).
* They can select drink options and sauces from a dropdown list.
* The order is compiled into a single, clean WhatsApp message showing all quantities, item details, and the calculated total price.

### C. Personalization Modal (Toppings, Allergies & Notes)
* **Crucial for kitchen efficiency and avoiding errors:**
* Clicking on any item opens a customization overlay:
  * *Remove toppings (Senza...)*: Deselect ingredients (e.g., unchecking onions adds `❌ SENZA cipolla` to the message).
  * *Add extras*: Check paid extras (e.g., `➕ Aggiungi mozzarella (+€1.00)`).
  * *Notes / Allergies*: A text field for custom requests like "Gluten allergy" or "Well-done meat".

### D. Table QR Code Ordering System (Dine-In)
* Every table in the pizzeria gets a unique QR code (e.g., `site.com/menu/?tavolo=5`).
* When scanned, the site automatically detects the table number. The final WhatsApp order reads: `Vorrei ordinare al Tavolo 5: ...`. Waiters only need to deliver the food, saving massive order-taking time.

### E. Google Maps Review QR Code (Cash Register)
* A dedicated desk stand flyer (generated at `assets/qr_review_flyer.png`) placed next to the cash register: *„Lasciaci una recensione su Google / Aiutaci a crescere! ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐”*.
* Customers scan the QR code while paying to instantly open the Google Maps review page for the pizzeria and leave a 5-star rating.

### F. Alternative to Complex POS Cash Register Systems (Kitchen Display via WhatsApp Web)
* **The Owner's Pain Point:** Updating menus and prices on touch-screen cash registers (cassa fiscale) in Italy is complicated and requires expensive authorized technician visits.
* **Your Solution:**
  * Install a simple All-in-One Touch PC or a large tablet on the kitchen wall running **WhatsApp Web** with zoomed text.
  * The chef reads detailed orders (with customizations: no onion, extra toppings) directly from the screen.
  * The physical cash register is used **only to input the final total price** and print the fiscal receipt (*scontrino*) when the customer pays. No more programming individual toppings or pizzas into the POS software!

## 4. Proposed Pricing Models (Post-Trial)

At the end of the trial month, you propose one of the following packages for both locations combined (Ceva + Garessio):

### Model 1: Pay-per-Order Commission - *Most Profitable*
* **Rate**: **€1 for every order** initiated via the website on WhatsApp.
* **Payment Security**:
  * *Prepaid Packages*: The client purchases order packages in advance (e.g., €100 for 100 orders; once depleted, order buttons temporarily disable until topped up).
  * *Volume-based Flat Fee*: If the trial shows ~200 orders/month, convert the commission into a predictable flat fee (e.g., **€150/month flat** for both locations).

### Model 2: Hybrid Package (One-off Setup + Small Flat Fee)
* **One-off Setup & Domain Fee**: **€150 - €180** (covers buying the `.it` domain for one year, DNS configuration, portal homepage creation, and the design of both QR code flyers).
* **Monthly Subscription**: **€25 - €30 / month** for both locations (covers hosting and unlimited menu/price updates).
