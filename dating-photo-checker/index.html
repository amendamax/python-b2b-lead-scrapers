10 Red Flags You're Talking to a Romance Scammer

The scammer will bombard you with affection and compliments ("love bombing") within days of starting the chat. The goal is to build emotional dependency before making financial requests.

Their webcam is always "broken", connection is "too weak", or they are constantly traveling. If someone refuses video chat in 2026, they are using stolen photos.

Their jobs justify why they cannot meet you. Typical roles include engineers on remote offshore rigs, military personnel deployed abroad, or doctors on foreign missions.

Suddenly, an emergency arises: they need urgent medical funds, their bank accounts are frozen, or they need cash for a plane ticket to see you. This is when the scam is triggered.
